Methodology

Basket & weighting

The composite is a weighted geometric mean of three sub-indices: GPU 60%, CPU 20%, RAM 20%. Each index is normalized so the first snapshot = 100; values below 100 mean compute has gotten cheaper since baseline.

Data sources

GPU rates from RunPod (Secure & Community Cloud) and Vast.ai (median ask). CPU and RAM unit prices decomposed from AWS EC2 c7i/r7i on-demand pricing into $/vCPU·hr and $/GB·hr.

Caveats

Prices are auto-collected on-demand list prices; they exclude spot/reserved discounts, egress, and storage. Marketplace medians can shift with inventory mix, not just price changes.
